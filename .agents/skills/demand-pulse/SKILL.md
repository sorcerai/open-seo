# OpenSEO Demand Pulse

## Goal

Turn live first-party, search, community, market-event, and AI-surface evidence
into a ranked set of prompt/problem families and concrete business actions.

## Evidence order

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. Human-locked customer prompts and first-party evidence.
2. Search Console, site search, support, sales, call, and product language.
3. Observed AI prompts, fan-out, answers, and citations.
4. Search-observed evidence: Suggest, PAA, Trends, SERP discussions/forums.
5. Community, reviews, issues, and specialist forums.
6. Competitor observations.
7. Generated variants, which are candidates only and cannot self-promote.

Never merge these classes into one unlabeled metric.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the OpenSEO project. Do not guess when several projects match.
2. Pull the 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day pulse.
3. Start with first-party observations and already-corroborated families.
4. Inspect source diversity, recurrence, normalized velocity, business fit,
   coverage gap, and penalties. Raw upvotes are not demand.
5. Collapse true duplicates, but preserve independent corroboration and mark
   likely cross-posts.
6. Validate promising families with search evidence and existing page coverage.
7. Assign one primary action:
   - update an existing page
   - add FAQ/comparison/troubleshooting content
   - create a tool or calculator
   - update product, offer, support, or sales enablement
   - monitor
   - reject
8. Require explicit confirmation before promotion or dismissal.
9. Record the scoring version and evidence URLs in every recommendation.
10. After implementation, measure GSC, visibility/citation, engagement, leads,
    and conversions. A page existing is not an outcome, despite the internet's
    heroic commitment to counting output instead of results.

## Promotion guard

A family normally needs one of:

- Two independent community sources.
- Community evidence plus search evidence.
- Community evidence plus first-party evidence.
- One exceptional first-party signal.
- A time-sensitive event override approved by a human.

Generated candidates never satisfy this gate by themselves.

## Output

Start with:

- Highest-value family.
- Why it matters now.
- Evidence classes and confidence.
- Existing coverage.
- Recommended action and target URL.
- Main risk or uncertainty.

Then provide a compact table:

| Family | Regime | Priority | Confidence | Evidence | Coverage | Action |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |

End with owners, next actions, and measurement criteria.
